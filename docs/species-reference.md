# Species Reference

Quick reference for the six leguminous cover crop species supported. Full citations are in `data-sources.md`.

## Mucuna bracteata (MB)

Lead species for mature oil palm and mature rubber due to deep rooting and shade tolerance.

- Typical seed rate: 2 to 3 kg/ha as monoculture; 1 to 2 kg/ha in mixtures.
- Establishment to full ground cover: 16 to 24 weeks.
- Biomass: 8 to 12 t/ha/year dry matter in mature stands.
- Nitrogen fixation: 150 to 200 kg N/ha/year (MPOB midpoint).
- Notes: aggressive climber; needs careful management near tapping panels in rubber.

## Pueraria javanica / Pueraria phaseoloides (PJ)

Workhorse legume for immature plantations across Southeast Asia and the humid tropics.

- Typical seed rate: 3 to 4 kg/ha as monoculture; 1 to 2 kg/ha in PORIM four-species mix.
- Establishment: 12 to 16 weeks.
- Biomass: 5 to 8 t/ha/year dry matter.
- Nitrogen fixation: 100 to 140 kg N/ha/year.
- Notes: needs full sun in establishment; declines under heavy canopy.

## Centrosema pubescens (CP)

Standard component of PORIM four-species mix. Persistent and palatable; doubles as livestock forage.

- Typical seed rate: 1 to 2 kg/ha in mixtures.
- Establishment: 12 to 16 weeks.
- Biomass: 4 to 6 t/ha/year dry matter.
- Nitrogen fixation: 80 to 130 kg N/ha/year.
- Notes: tolerates moderate shade better than Pueraria.

## Calopogonium mucunoides (CM)

Fast-establishing component of the standard mix. Short-lived; supplies rapid early ground cover then yields ground to longer-lived species.

- Typical seed rate: 2 to 3 kg/ha in mixtures.
- Establishment: 8 to 12 weeks.
- Biomass: 3 to 5 t/ha/year dry matter while present.
- Nitrogen fixation: 70 to 110 kg N/ha/year.
- Notes: declines after year two; relies on natural reseeding.

## Calopogonium caeruleum (CC)

Longer-lived Calopogonium. Useful where the mucunoides component fails or in mixed plantings.

- Typical seed rate: 1 to 2 kg/ha in mixtures.
- Establishment: 12 to 16 weeks.
- Biomass: 3 to 5 t/ha/year dry matter.
- Nitrogen fixation: 60 to 100 kg N/ha/year.

## Standard plantation mixtures

- **PORIM four-species mix:** PJ + CP + CM + CC at total rate 6 to 10 kg/ha. Use for immature oil palm and young rubber.
- **Mucuna-led mature mix:** MB dominant, optional CP secondary, at total rate 2 to 4 kg/ha. Use for mature oil palm and mature rubber.
- **Smallholder budget mix:** PJ + CM at total rate 4 to 6 kg/ha. Lower cost, accepts higher year-on-year reseeding effort.
